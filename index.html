Our Story

Founded in 2021, Aurea Concierge was created from a passion for transforming ideas into beautifully planned, memorable experiences. What began as a vision to make event planning more seamless has grown into a concierge service dedicated to bringing together thoughtful details.

At Aurea, we believe that every event has its own story. Whether it's a wedding or any other celebration, we take the time to understand the purpose, personality and vision behind each occasion.

The first step to your perfect event

We meet with you to understand your vision, your style, your occasion, your guests, and the experience you want to create. From weddings and private celebrations to corporate and professional events, Aurea Concierge helps you bring every detail together beautifully and stress-free.

  1. Event Direction & Inspiration

    A clear brief capturing your event style, theme, priorities, guest experience, décor and special requirements.

  2. Venue Recommendations & Outsourcing Vendors

    Carefully selected venue suggestions based on your event type, number of guests, style, location, and budget.

  3. Cake Recommendations

    Guidance and recommendations for cake styles, designs, flavours, and cake providers that suit your celebration.

  4. Photography Recommendations

    Recommendations for photographers and ideas for capturing the important moments of your event.

  5. Expert Planning & Budget Guidance

    Professional recommendations to help you prioritize your budget and plan your event efficiently.
